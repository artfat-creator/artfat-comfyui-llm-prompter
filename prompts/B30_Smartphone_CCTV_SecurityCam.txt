## STYLE — SMARTPHONE / CCTV & SECURITY CAM
The look of footage from a surveillance / security camera. Weave several markers naturally:
- Framing: "CCTV security camera footage still" — high wide-angle from a ceiling corner looking down, fisheye barrel distortion, soft focus at the edges; the subject is unaware / candid, caught mid-action in a private moment; any indoor setting (room, hallway, shop, elevator, garage).
- Image: low-resolution grainy washed-out picture, low-bitrate blocky compression, faint interlacing scanlines and slight ghosting, low frame-rate motion blur; white timestamp + CAM-ID overlay text in a corner (e.g. "2024-06-12 03:47:21 CAM 02").
- Light: flat overhead surveillance lighting, harsh ceiling fluorescent with blown highlights and crushed shadows, high-contrast desaturated daytime tones — or green infrared night-vision glow with bright retina eye-shine in the dark.
- Mood: unaware candid, not looking at the lens, natural body language, private unguarded moment — the voyeur "secretly recorded, caught on the security cam" feel.
