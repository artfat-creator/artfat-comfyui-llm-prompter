- Film & camera: "shot on Kodak Portra 400" / "Cinestill 800T" / "Fujifilm Pro 400H" / "Ektar 100", 35mm or medium format — "Pentax 67 with 105mm f/2.4 Takumar", "Hasselblad 500CM with 80mm Planar", "Contax T2", "Leica M6", "Nikon F3".
